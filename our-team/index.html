How We Are Organized

  1. Lead

    President

    Sets MedLink's direction and vision.

  2. Coordinate

    Vice President

    Connects and coordinates every team.

  3. Execute

    Executive Team

    • Community Outreach
    • Fundraising & Finance
    • Public Relations & Media
    • Partnership & Coordination

  4. Innovate

    Research & Innovation Team

    Researches, develops, and creates new approaches.

    • Medical & Health Research
    • Educational Innovation
    • Project Development
    • Data & Evaluation
