Beyond Thailand

In 2026, MedLink partnered with MamaCare Ghana, a non-profit working for the health of mothers, newborns and children in underserved communities. Money raised by our students paid for nutrition and malaria teaching materials for 10 community health nurses and health workers in Biakoye District, in Ghana's Oti Region.

The health workers use them to teach mothers and caregivers about breastfeeding, feeding young children, hygiene and growth, and to help families spot the danger signs of malaria, get treatment early and stop mosquitoes from breeding.

  • Partner: MamaCare Ghana
  • Biakoye District, Oti Region
