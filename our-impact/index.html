Beyond Thailand

In summer 2026, MedLink partnered with MamaCare, an NGO in Ghana, and gave financial support to people in need.

MamaCare used the funding to produce nutrition and malaria teaching materials for 10 community health workers in Biakoye District, in Ghana's Oti Region.
