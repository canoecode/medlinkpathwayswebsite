About Research & Innovation

MedLink members build medical devices and run research aimed at screening that is earlier, cheaper, and easier to reach. Some projects started with problems we saw on outreach trips. We also prepare members for science competitions such as YSC (NSTDA) and ISEF.

  • Pulma

    Estimates lung cancer risk from a breath sample

    iCAN Gold Medal

  • TeleSync

    Portable check-ups for patients far from a hospital

    Gold Medal & Jury's Choice, iCAN 2026

  • RememoCare

    Looks for early signs of Alzheimer's with EEG, paired with music therapy

    Top 3, KU MedAI 2026

  • EpivaScan

    AI skin screening for highland farming communities

  • Thermography Study

    Infrared imaging study with 120+ pregnant women

  • ORADENT

    Checks breath for early signs of gum disease, using a gas sensor and AI

    1st Place, Bosch INNOVISION 2026

  • Nematode Biocontrol

    Trains a natural pest-killer to replace chemical pesticides
