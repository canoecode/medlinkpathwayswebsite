  • Patthanan Uthaititpitak

    Co-Founder, Vice President 2024 to 2025

    Ouk served as the vice president of Medlink Pathways from 2024 to 2025, during which his role was to take the organisation's planned policies and allocate tasks to making it practical. Ouk's main contributions to the organization included accounting and finance work for the 2025 Valentine's Day fundraiser to raise money for the Doi Water Reservoir Project; leading the organisation in reception, translation, and Doi fundraising advertisements at the 10th ASEAN MISST conference; volunteer work preparing steroid injections and reception work at the Amphor Mae Chaem clinic; and hosting an interactive marshmallow tower building game to teach the scientific method to over 30 elementary students at Saint Joseph Mae Chaem School.
