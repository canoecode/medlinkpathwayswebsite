TeleSync

Portable. Connected. Bilingual.

A portable exam kit that lets doctors check patients who can't easily reach a hospital.

The Problem

Many rural and homebound patients in Thailand can't travel to a hospital for regular checks.

[Rural access statistic]

How It Works

  1. 1

    Step 1: The kit is used in the patient's home.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Sensors measure heart rate, blood pressure, and blood oxygen (SpO2), and the kit records heart and lung sounds.

    ESP32 microcontroller · MAX30102 pulse and oxygen sensor

  3. 3

    Step 3: Readings go to a Thai and English app and a dashboard for doctors.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Results feed into the record system used by state hospitals, so information isn't lost between visits.

Key Features

  • Vital signs plus heart and lung sounds in one kit

  • Thai and English app with a doctor dashboard

  • Linked to state hospital records
