Pulma

Non-invasive. Affordable. Early.

A device that estimates lung cancer risk from a breath sample.

The Problem

[CONTENT TO COME: one sentence on lung cancer in Thailand]

How It Works

  1. 1

    Step 1: The person breathes into the device.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Sensors measure volatile organic compounds (VOCs) in the breath.

  3. 3

    Step 3: A machine learning model reads the key markers, mainly aldehydes and alkanes, and gives a risk score.

  4. 4

    Step 4: People with a high score know to get a full check at a hospital.

Key Features

  • Breath sample only, no needles or radiation

  • Built for low-cost screening

  • Flags who needs a full check
