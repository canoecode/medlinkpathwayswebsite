Thermography Study

Cutaneous thermal asymmetry as a marker of sympathetically mediated vascular dysfunction in pregnant women

Infrared imaging for low-back pain in pregnancy.

Summary

We studied whether skin temperature differences between the left and right side of the back can show nerve-driven changes in blood flow. We measured them with an infrared camera. The aim was to test thermal imaging as a quick, low-cost first check for low-back pain in pregnancy, since CT uses radiation and MRI is expensive.

  • 120+ pregnant women scanned
  • 3 hospitals
  • 2025
