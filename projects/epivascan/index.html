EpivaScan

Skin screening from a single photo.

An AI app that screens photos of skin for 8 diseases, built for farming communities in the highlands.

EpivaScan began in 2024 as our Skin & AI project, after we saw how common skin conditions were on our first Mae Chaem trip.

The Problem

Skin conditions are common in Mae Chaem's highland villages, where the nearest hospital is hours away.

8

skin diseases covered

How It Works

  1. 1

    Step 1: A photo of the affected skin is taken in the app.

  2. 2

    Step 2: A vision transformer model, trained on 40,000+ images of skin disease, checks it against 8 conditions.

  3. 3

    Step 3: The app shows the most likely condition.

Key Features

  • Trained on 40,000+ images

  • 80% accuracy across 8 diseases

  • Full-stack app built for wide distribution
